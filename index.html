JOS, PLATEAU STATE

Imagine waking up to cool weather, with mist hanging gently over the hills as you take a quiet morning walk or run. The air feels fresh, the scenery seems almost unreal, and life seems to move at a slower pace than you will find in other cities in Nigeria.Let me tell you all about this beautiful city - Jos.

Background

Jos, popularly known as “J-Town,” is the capital and largest city of Plateau State in North-Central Nigeria. It is located on the scenic Jos Plateau and surrounded by the Beautiful Guinea Savannah Landscape. The city is known for its cool climate, winding mountain roads, and beautiful landscapes.

Interesting facts about Jos

  1. Cool climate and dramatic landscape: Jos is known for its cool weather, misty mornings, rolling hills and rocky landscapes. Locals sometimes claim to have seen “snow,” which is probably hailstorm and thick fog adding to the city’s reputation for having one of Nigeria’s coolest climates.
  2. Ancient Nok connection: The region is linked to the ancient Nok culture, which flourished in central Nigeria more than 2,000 years ago and is famous for its distinctive terracotta sculptures.
  3. Tin mining history: Jos became an important mining centre following the discovery of large tin deposits in the early 20th century, which played a major role in the city’s development.
  4. Cultural diversity: Jos is home to people from diverse ethnic and cultural backgrounds, giving the city a rich mix of traditions, languages, food and festivals.

Why visit Jos?

  • The weather is mostly cool and crisp for the larger part of the year. You will enjoy fresh mountain air, misty mornings and temperatures that make you want to take a long walk and stay outside a little longer.
  • Jos has a lot of tourist attractions such as the rugged beauty of Shere Hills, waterfalls and other beautiful places across the Plateau, there’s always somewhere new to explore.
  • Jos is culturally diverse and the people are known to be warm, friendly and welcoming.
  • With farming communities all around the Plateau, Jos is blessed with fresh and affordable local produce including crisp vegetables and fruits.
